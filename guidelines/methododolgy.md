## Method of work

- No Authentication for ordinary users
- Authentication for companies or drivers or Mechanics or Admins
- The ordinary commuters can request emergency service by phone number
- Location detection by GPS for both ordinary commuters and companies to determine the closeness and routing of drivers based on closeness
- Companies can add their vehicles to the system
- Drivers can add their vehicles to the system
- When a vehicle is enroute, the driver's number and phone will be used to track the TOA
- pricing is calculated based on distance and type of vehicle. This is calculated on the server side and sent to both driver and prospective customer/ordinary user
